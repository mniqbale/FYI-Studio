- **Docker Compose:** `postgres:15-alpine` + `redis:7-alpine`, healthchecks + named volumes, mapped to host ports 5432/6379.
- **Dev credentials:** `postgres/postgres`, DB `fyi_studio` (local-only, documented in `.env.example`).
