## Decisions Made

- **Schema source of truth:** Implemented the authoritative `jobs` + `telemetry` tables from `.ai/architecture/contracts.md` (§1.4, §1.6), NOT the outdated draft in `Issue-002.md`. Differences: `JobStatus` includes `WAITING_APPROVAL`; `jobs` includes `tenant_id` + `current_step_index`; `telemetry` includes `worker_id`, `worker_version`, `provider`, `model`, `tokens_in/out`, `seconds`, `cost`, `duration_ms` + all indexes.
- **Prisma 5.22** (aligned with approved stack / SYSTEM.md "Prisma 5.x").
- **UUID PK** on `jobs` (`@db.Uuid`), autoincrement Int PK on `telemetry` (matches `id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY` in contracts.md).
- **JSONB** via Prisma `Json` type for `recipe_snapshot` and `artifacts`.
- **Singleton PrismaClient** pattern with globalThis caching + env-based log level.
- **pnpm build-script allowlist** in `pnpm-workspace.yaml` (`onlyBuiltDependencies`) to permit prisma/esbuild postinstall.
