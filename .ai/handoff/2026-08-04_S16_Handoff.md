## Decisions Made

- **Contracts v1.1 authoritative:** NOT the outdated Issue-006 draft (used `WorkerCapability` enum, `supervisor.startJob()` which doesn't exist, and telemetry fields `capability`/`tokens_used`/`cost_estimate`/`timestamp` that aren't in the v1.1 schema).
- **Artifacts assertions match actual schema:** artifacts are nested per step id (`artifacts.research`, `.script`, `.voice`) + `_references` (not the flat `research_brief`/`script` shape in the draft).
- **Telemetry assertions match actual schema:** `worker_id`, `cost`, `duration_ms`, `created_at` (not `capability`/`tokens_used`/`timestamp`).
- **Pipeline via child processes:** the test spawns the 3 workers + supervisor (like the CLI) rather than importing a nonexistent `startJob()`.
- **Failure-path test** added per spec ("intentionally break a mock worker"): an unresolvable capability causes the supervisor to mark the job FAILED.
- **`tests/` as a workspace member** so `@fyi/*` deps resolve (glob is `tests`, not `tests/*`).
